# Platinum Arena0.9.3 polish demo

Unzip the download and open Platinum-Arena-0.9.3-POLISH.nds in your Nintendo DS emulator. The ROM is256 MiB.

Adds the new effects, six creator bases, all493 directional Pokemon, held shields, chip damage, beam clashes, larger arena and corrected menus. HoldL+R+Select at the title screen for the1v1/2v2 Battle Lab.

This demo retains pending final story/reload checks in RELEASE.json. The earlier preview passed those checks; full campaign/hardware validation remains open. Preserve your in-game save and import/copy it under the new ROM filename to continue.
