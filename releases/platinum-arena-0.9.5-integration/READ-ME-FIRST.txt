PLATINUM ARENA 0.9.5 — INTEGRATION PLAYTEST BUILD

This is the native Platinum-based game with live-action combat. It is not a
standalone Emerald ROM or a 3DS executable. It has compiled and linked; this
exact build is being delivered for YOUR gameplay testing, not marked as tested.

PLAY
1. Extract Platinum-Arena-0.9.5-INTEGRATION.zip.
2. Open Platinum-Arena-0.9.5-INTEGRATION.nds in your Nintendo DS emulator.
3. Keep a backup of your existing save before trying a new development build.
4. Play the Platinum story normally, or open Battle Lab from the title screen
   by holding L + R + SELECT. Battle Lab uses temporary teams and discards
   practice HP/PP/rewards when you leave.

DEFAULT LIVE-COMBAT CONTROLS
D-pad: move; double-tap a direction: dash; Y: equipped move 1;
A: equipped move 2; X: equipped move 3 or 4; L: toggle X between 3/4;
B held while stationary: shield; B + direction: dodge; SELECT: aim lock;
START: pause/party/bag/controls. L + R: catch in supported wild encounters.
B is fixed to defence. R remains available outside the catch chord.

SCOPE
This pass updates equipped-move controls, double-tap dashing, interactive
beam pressure, move-specific Swift artwork, contact-driven impact visuals
and live-combat presentation. Expanded layered character creation and
global Pokemon followers are not integrated in this build.
Exact implementation details and remaining limits
are in the accompanying changelog and RELEASE.json.

The source changes are supplied as a separate download. New source files are
enough for development; no further base ROM upload is required.

TESTING
Gameplay validation, including story entry/return/save, 1v1/2v2, controls,
sound, collision feel and visual timing, remains pending your playtesting.
Compiler success does not establish these behaviors.

Artwork credits and source notices are retained in credits/.
